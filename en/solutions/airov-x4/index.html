AIROV X4Move Freely. Navigate Intelligently. Operate Safely.

An AI-powered autonomous mobile robot for indoor and outdoor environments, combining four-wheel independent drive, 180° independent steering on each axis, 3D LiDAR-based multi-sensor perception and real-time safety control.

One autonomous platform connecting indoor production with outdoor logistics.

Each of the four wheels is independently driven and steered. 3D LiDAR and ultrasonic sensors perceive the surroundings and detect obstacles, while touch sensors support contact protection. The AI Navigation System analyzes the environment and plans an optimal route to the destination.

01

Four-Wheel Independent Drive

Four 750 W servo motors independently control forward, reverse, rotational and diagonal motion.

02

180° Independent Steering

Independent steering on each axis supports omnidirectional motion and turning in confined spaces.

03

AI Multi-Sensor Navigation

3D LiDAR and ultrasonic sensing support environmental perception and route planning.

04

Industrial Safety Control

Four emergency stop switches complement real-time deceleration and stop control.

CORE TECHNOLOGY

Core Technology

Drive, steering, perception and safety control integrated into one autonomous mobile system.

01

Four-Wheel Independent Drive

Four 750 W servo motors and precision gear reducers with a 1:70 ratio enable independent direction and speed control on each drive axis.

02

180° Independent Steering

Independent 180° steering on each axis provides omnidirectional motion through four-axis directional control, supporting confined-space turns and precise positioning.

Omnidirectional motionConfined-space turningPrecise approach
03

AI Multi-Sensor Navigation

3D LiDARUltrasonic sensorsAI Navigation System

Industrial Safety Architecture
