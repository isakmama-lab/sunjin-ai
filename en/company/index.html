CEO MESSAGE

Understanding Your Process.
Engineering Practical Change.

Guided by our philosophy, Happiness via Technology, we work to solve industrial challenges through engineering. Since 2014, we have designed and built automation equipment and robotic systems for steelworks, heavy industry, automotive and manufacturing applications.

We look closely at each customer's process and integrate mechanics, controls, robots, sensors and software to support productivity, consistent quality and safe working environments.

We will continue to research and validate practical technologies and work with our customers toward sustainable smart manufacturing.

LEE WOO SONG, CEO
